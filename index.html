THE UN'S GOAL TO END POVERTY:

The eradication of poverty for a better tomorrow.

Globally 1/10 people still suffer from extreme poverty. The minimization of this statistic, or, even better, its complete eradication is the goal of the United Nations.
This site is dedicated to; Spreading awareness for this causes, informing you, the user what you can do to help and the real facts of the matter.
Keep exploring the site to educate yourself, and join the fight to end global poverty today.
